Neighborhood by neighborhood: where Ashland takes on water

Ashland's draft hazard mitigation plan, updated in 2025 by the Metropolitan Area Planning Council, names specific trouble spots. The Megunko Hill Stream floods the Tilton and Cherry Street area, including the fire station, and the plan calls it the biggest flooding nuisance in downtown Ashland.

The same plan lists Central and Alden Streets, Metcalf Avenue at the railroad tracks, Oak and Oregon Streets, Edgewood Drive, the Concord Street bridge area on the Sudbury River, Olive Street near Esther Lane, and the South Street boat landing. If your street is on that list, keep a plan for storm days. If it is not, a pipe failure can still put you in the same position as anyone else.

The plan names March 2010 as the most severe recent flooding in the region. It also counts 54 flood events in Middlesex County between 2010 and 2022. For a homeowner, the lesson is that repeated moderate storms matter as much as one big one, because materials that dry out slowly can stay damp between events.

Sewer backups in a mostly sewered town

Ashland's housing production plan says public sewer serves about 91 percent of the town's housing units, and the wastewater flows into the MWRA system. That sounds like good news, and it mostly is. It also means the most common sewage problem is a backup in a shared line, not a failed septic tank.

If a drain backs up, stop using water in the house and call the Department of Public Works about the line. Cleanup of that water is a Category 3 job, meaning contaminated, and our crews wear protective gear and remove porous materials rather than trying to dry them.

Water, wells and what comes out of the tap

Ashland's water does not come from the reservoirs people see from the road. The town's 2023 water quality report says five groundwater wells supply it under normal conditions, with the MWRA as a backup through an interconnection with Southborough. Ozone aeration treats iron and manganese.

If a pipe breaks, the water on your floor is treated tap water, usually clean at first. Clean water turns into a bigger problem after about two days in a wall cavity, so quick extraction and drying through water mitigation keep the job in the lower-cost category.

Local landmarks and why they matter to a leak

Ashland State Park surrounds the 470-acre Ashland Reservoir, run by the state's conservation agency, and Hopkinton State Park sits on the Ashland and Hopkinton line. Reservoirs and their dams are rated by the state for downstream hazard, and the plan lists six dams in Ashland. A hazard rating describes a failure's effect, not a dam's condition, so check the FEMA flood map for your address instead of guessing.

Ashland was the original starting point of the Boston Marathon, and the race route follows Union Street through the center. On race weekends, traffic changes how fast a crew can move. That is one more reason we avoid promising arrival times.

Permits and offices in Ashland

Inspectional Services at 101 Main Street in Town Hall handles building permits and links to the FEMA flood map lookup. The Conservation Department enforces the town's wetlands protection bylaw. Rules vary by property, so ask both before a rebuild near a stream, the reservoir or a wetland.
