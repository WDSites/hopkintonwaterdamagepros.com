What this site is

Hopkinton Water Damage Pros operates a 24/7 dispatch line for water damage and mold restoration in Hopkinton, Massachusetts and nearby towns. The website explains our services and the areas we cover. It is general information, not a diagnosis of your property or a substitute for an on-site assessment.

How service works

When you call, we describe your situation to licensed and insured restoration professionals who may be sent to your property. The scope of work and price are explained before work starts, and you decide whether to proceed. Work is performed under the agreement you make with the company on site.

Crew availability changes with demand, weather and traffic. We do not promise a specific arrival time. Restoration results depend on the type of water, how long materials stayed wet and the condition of the building.
