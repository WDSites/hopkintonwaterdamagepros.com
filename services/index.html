Water Damage Services in Hopkinton, MA

Six services, one 24/7 line. Pick the stage you are at, or call and describe what you see.

Water damage work happens in stages: stop the water, dry the structure, clean what was affected, and rebuild. Each stage has its own page below. If you are not sure which one you need, call (508) 446-9244 and describe what you see. The line is open 24/7, and the scope and price are explained before work starts.

For a full overview of how the stages fit together, start with our water damage restoration homepage.

Choose a service

  • Water Damage Repair: Rebuilding drywall, ceilings, floors and paint once the structure is dry.
  • Flood Damage Restoration: Basement flooding, storm water and sewage backups, treated as contaminated water.
  • Water Mitigation: Moisture mapping, air movers and dehumidifiers to stop the damage from spreading.
  • Water Damage Cleanup: Removing ruined materials, sanitizing and deodorizing after the water stops.
  • Emergency Water Removal: Pumps and extraction units for standing water from burst pipes and failures.
  • Mold Remediation: Finding the moisture, containing the area and removing mold growth.
